About 自己紹介

My research interests include Category Theory, Homological Algebra, and Representation Theory of Rings. In particular, I am interested in DG-algebras, DG-categories, and extriangulated categories. Recently, I have been interested in extended module categories of DG-algebras, higher homological algebra, and the abstract categorical structures associated with them.

圏論，ホモロジー代数，環の表現論に関心があります．特に DG代数，DG圏，Extriangulated圏の理論に興味があります． 最近は，DG代数の拡大加群圏や高次ホモロジー代数，およびそれらに付随する抽象的な圏構造に興味があります．
